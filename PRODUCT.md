# NR Studios

## Register

brand

## Users

Fashion-conscious visitors exploring Indian sarees, and prospective clients evaluating this flagship portfolio storefront on mobile and desktop.

## Product Purpose

Make discovering a saree feel like entering a considered atelier. The signature hanging rack leads into an editorial product experience and a usable catalog and shopping bag.

## Brand Personality

Regal, intimate, contemporary. The user's supplied direction is modern Indian royal heritage crossed with international luxury fashion.

## Anti-references

Generic ecommerce templates, excessive ornament, boxed feature cards, tiny illegible labels, flat image scaling masquerading as fabric movement, invented endorsements and unconfigured transactions presented as live services.

## Design Principles

- Keep the cloth physically attached while browsing; make detachment intentional during the reveal.
- Let sarees and their matching portraits carry the story.
- Preserve the existing NR monogram and established assets.
- Make every destination and interaction work, including interruption and return navigation.
- Clearly distinguish portfolio sample data from live inventory or contact services.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic navigation, visible focus, keyboard equivalents, readable contrast, touch targets, vertical scrolling, pinch zoom, and reduced-motion support. Test widths from 360px through desktop.
